## Brand & Style
The design system reflects a couture beauty sanctuary and certified vocational academy rooted in heritage since 1998. The aesthetic bridges classic Punjabi regal hospitality with sleek editorial minimalism. It balances two key identities: the intimate indulgence of a high-end salon/bridal studio, and the structured authority of a premier beauty academy.

The visual direction uses a hybrid of **Modern Editorial Luxury** and **Subtle Luminous Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep velvet intimacy offset by pale ice-silver luminosity, evoking polished platinum mirrors, velvet upholstery, and warm champagne-gold trim.
- **Tone:** Prestigious, inclusive, warm, master-tier, and disciplined.
- **Affirming Spaces:** Subtle, refined inclusion badges (LGBTQ+ friendly, bridal bespoke suites) integrated into the layout as understated luxury marks rather than generic corporate decals.

## Colors
The palette balances deep pigment depth against crystalline lightness to avoid both dark heaviness and sterile clinical whiteness.

- **Primary (`#3A0F34` - Deep Royal Plum):** Used for commanding headlines, signature interactive elements, heavy editorial dividers, and anchor buttons.
- **Secondary (`#EAAA00` - Warm Regal Gold):** Reserved for delicate metallic borders, active indicator stars, masterclass accreditations, award ribbons, and focal hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#4A3E4E` - Muted Plum Slate):** Subdued body paragraphs, subtle icons, input borders, and instructional copy.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#DDE3E8` - Ice Silver):** The base atmospheric wash. Creates a chilled silver mirror reflection that softens stark contrasts.
- **Supporting Layers:**
  - Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated cards, masterclass syllabus sheets, and pristine booking modal containers.
  - Deepest Velvet (`#20061D`): Deep hero surfaces, footer foundations, and high-impact contrast panels.
  - Warm Whipped Alabaster (`#F7F5F8`): Secondary card containers and bridal service panels.

## Typography
The typographic rhythm juxtaposes high-contrast editorial serifs (`Playfair Display`) with an approachable, clean geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

- **Display & Headings:** Always set in `Playfair Display`. Titles capture the heritage and high-fashion nuance of classic vogue covers and bridal lookbooks. For titles emphasizing certifications or academy heritage, italicized accents within the serif may be used sparingly.
- **Body & Subtitles:** Handled by `Plus Jakarta Sans` to guarantee effortless legibility for course curricula, treatment breakdowns, and appointment scheduling.
- **Overlines & Badges:** Use `label-lg` or `label-md` in uppercase with wide letter tracking (`0.08em` - `0.12em`), providing a boutique, tailored finish.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display large, breathable imagery (bridal portraits, studio interiors, editorial transformations) alongside concise course modules.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. High-priority cards (Booking, Masterclasses) stack into clean 1-column spreads with horizontal scroll strips for service categories.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin-md: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Editorial side-by-side treatments (e.g., service on the left, high-res treatment imagery on the right).
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width `1360px`, `margin-lg: 5rem`, `gutter-lg: 2rem`. Asymmetrical editorial magazine layouts, wide whitespace cushions, and pinned appointment booking sidebars.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics the natural refraction of mirror glass, lacquer tables, and deep brushed brass fixtures.

- **Depth Strategy:** Tonal layering reinforced by frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and gentle warm plum ambient shadows instead of harsh black drops.
- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Flat Ice Silver (`#DDE3E8`).
  - **Elevated Surfaces (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F5F8` with a micro-border `rgba(58, 15, 52, 0.08)` and shadow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(32, 6, 29, 0.05)`.
  - **Interactive Overlays (Level 2 - Modals, Floating Consult Bar):** White or deep royal plum frosted glass with shadow `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(32, 6, 29, 0.16)`.
- **Metallic Borders:** Thin 1px borders using `rgba(234, 170, 0, 0.35)` to define luxury packages, bridal tiers, and certified diploma badges.

## Shapes
A subtle soft radius (Level 1 / 4px base) ensures precision and architectural structure while avoiding severe industrial sharpness. 

- **Containers & Cards:** Use subtle rounding (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem` for large cards) that mirrors stone trays and sleek vanity mirrors.
- **Service Tags & Chips:** Slightly softened corners without turning fully into bubble pills, maintaining an elite bespoke feel.
- **Imagery:** Strict rectangular frames paired occasionally with arched upper borders (`border-top-left-radius: 999px; border-top-right-radius: 999px;`) for feature bridal displays and academy faculty portraits.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Book Consultation", "Enroll in Academy"):** Background `#3A0F34`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#3A0F34`. On hover: background `#20061D`, border-color `#EAAA00`, text `#EAAA00`. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Letter spacing: `0.06em` uppercase.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Explore Curriculum", "View Gallery"):** Ghost style with `#3A0F34` outline (1px), transparent background, text `#3A0F34`. On hover: background `#3A0F34`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Luxury Accent CTA (e.g., "Bespoke Bridal Suite"):** Gold tint `#EAAA00` with dark velvet text `#20061D`, transition into radiant gold shadow `0 4px 18px rgba(234, 170, 0, 0.35)`.

### Cards & Service Tiers
- **Service & Treatment Cards:** White background `#FFFFFF`, padded with `space-lg`, micro-borders in `#DDE3E8`. Features price tags in deep royal plum and duration in muted slate.
- **Academy Course Cards:** Light alabaster surface `#F7F5F8` framed with a subtle gold top border (`2px solid #EAAA00`). Highlights certification level, hands-on hour badges, and syllabus download action.

### Badges & Micro-Signatures
- **Since 1998 Heritage Crest:** Minimalist circular gold outline with micro-serif typography.
- **Inclusive & LGBTQ+ Affirming Badge:** Refined micro-pill with a muted plum outline, soft translucent plum background (`rgba(58, 15, 52, 0.05)`), and a discreet chromatic gold star pip.

### Inputs & Booking Forms
- **Fields:** Clean underlines or bordered boxes with `#F7F5F8` background, 1px border in `#4A3E4E` (30% opacity). Active focus states transition smoothly to `#3A0F34` with an inner 1px gold glow line (`#EAAA00`).
- **Typography in Inputs:** `body-md` in `Plus Jakarta Sans` for direct readability when choosing dates, hair lengths, and bridal party counts.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square custom marks with `0.125rem` radius. Inactive borders in `#4A3E4E`. Checked state fills with `#3A0F34` containing a sharp `#EAAA00` metallic check icon.
